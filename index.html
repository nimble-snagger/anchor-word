How to play Anchor Word

Goal

Build the biggest tally of points you can before you run out of discards.

Each turn

You're given a word. You either place it on the grid or discard it. A discard costs 1 point and adds a mark to the right-hand margin.

Starting out

The game begins with one word in the middle of the grid, inside a gold box. This boxed area is your anchor.

Placing a word

  1. Tap a letter in your word.
  2. The matching letters inside the box light up. Tap one.
  3. Use the rotate buttons to turn the word to any of the 8 directions (across, up, down or diagonal). Flip reverses it.
  4. Tap Place.

A placement is allowed if:

  • at least one letter lands on a matching letter inside the box,
  • any other letter that lands on an existing letter matches it, and
  • the whole word fits on the grid.

Like a word search, letters on the board don't need to form words once they're placed.

Scoring

You score 1 point for every letter of your word that lands on an existing letter, anywhere on the grid (not just inside the box). Points are marked in the top margin, in fives.

Growing the box

The box can grow in two ways:

  1. Place a word whose letters all land on matching letters already on the grid, with at least one inside the box. The box stretches to include them.
  2. When a whole side of the box is lined with letters and has no gaps, the box grows by itself to take them in, as far as the unbroken letters reach.

Growing the box scores no points, but it gives you more letters to connect to.

Ending the game

The game ends when the right-hand margin is full. That takes 108 discards on the standard grid. Your final score is your points minus your discards.

This is an early beta, so you may hit rough edges. Thanks for helping to test it.
